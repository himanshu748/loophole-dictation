The gap between
said and meant.

A rule can do exactly what you asked—and still surprise you. Loophole makes that gap visible.

  1. Give the world a rule.

    Speak naturally or start with an example. Review how your words become gate behavior.

  2. Look for the exception.

    The search explores possible moves and returns a concrete sequence that breaks your goal.

  3. Change a few words.

    Apply a revision. Test the original moves again and watch the outcome change.

What this world can—and cannot—model

Two robots, blue/red badges, one gate. Rules control the badge that opens it, who can use the opening, and whether a badge is checked again at crossing. Experiments optionally allow overtaking or badge revocation before entry. No timing, escorts, real hardware, physical collisions, or arbitrary exceptions. Search exhausts reachable model states when it finds no violation; it stops at the first shortest counterexample otherwise. Create a rubric to choose permitted badge colors, revocation requirements and the maximum number of robots inside. All selected criteria must hold.

Audio is sent to AssemblyAI when you finish recording or transcribe the sample. Original and cleaned transcripts are available for review. Custom rule text is sent to AssemblyAI’s LLM Gateway for interpretation. Rules and experiment history stay in this browser; exported evidence includes rule text and transcripts, not audio. The server does not save recordings. Built-in examples use predefined interpretations, visibly identified as examples.
