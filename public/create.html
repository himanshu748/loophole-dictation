    What happens to your words.
    AssemblyAI’s Dictation /transcribe endpoint returns your original speech and uses llm_instruction to extract the world, rules and rubric in the same request. You review that draft. AssemblyAI’s LLM Gateway then proposes a finite model or an AI critique.
    You approve a model before Loophole searches it locally. A result applies to that model and its stated assumptions. Open-ended critique remains an AI hypothesis. Your session stays in this page until you share or export it; the server does not save recordings.
