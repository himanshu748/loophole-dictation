A discount can be used twice. A document can change after approval. Say what should happen, then inspect what your rule actually permits.

  1. Say what should happen.

    Describe the world, its current rules, and your rubric. Review the structured draft extracted from your words.

  2. Find the move you missed.

    Search the possible actions or try them yourself. Concrete mechanics get a replayable model. Open-ended subjects get clearly labeled AI critique.

  3. Change the rule. Keep the evidence.

    Replay the original moves under your revision. See which move gets blocked and why.
